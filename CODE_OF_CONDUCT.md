# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Agent Shield is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, accept constructive feedback, and respect differing experiences and viewpoints.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, or other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact options on the [mason50x GitHub profile](https://github.com/mason50x). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
